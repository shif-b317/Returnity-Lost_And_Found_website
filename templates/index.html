How RETURNITY Works

A multi-tier verification process designed to reduce false claims and assist administrators with objective decision support.

01

Report with Split Information

When an item is reported, general details and safe photos are made public. Hidden markings, serial numbers, and private identifying details are held securely for ownership verification only.

02

Submit Verification Claim

Claimants must answer specific questions regarding private characteristics without seeing the answers. Optional purchase receipts or warranty proof can be securely attached.

03

Rule-Based Verification & Review

The system calculates a rule-based Claim Verification Score, flags potential contradictions or duplicate requests, and submits the claim to the administrator for final authorized approval.
